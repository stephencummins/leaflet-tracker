# Leaflet Tracker - Volunteer Guide

## Getting Started

1. Go to **roadie.stephen8n.com** on your phone or computer
2. Enter your name and tap **Let's Go**
3. You'll see the dashboard showing overall progress across all zones

Your name is remembered on your device so you won't need to enter it again.

## The Dashboard

The dashboard shows:

- **Progress ring** - percentage of the ward completed
- **Stats** - streets done, houses delivered, active volunteers
- **Zone cards** - tap any zone to see its streets

## Delivering Leaflets

1. Tap a **zone card** on the dashboard (or go to **Zones** in the bottom nav)
2. Find the street you want to deliver
3. Tap **Claim** to let others know you're taking that street (optional but helpful)
4. Go deliver the leaflets!
5. When you're done, tap **Done** to mark the street as complete

### Tips

- You don't have to claim a street before marking it done - you can just tap **Done** directly
- Use the **filter tabs** (All / To Do / Done) to quickly find undelivered streets
- If you claimed a street but can't do it, tap **Drop** to release it for someone else
- If a street was marked done by mistake, tap **Undo** to revert it

## The Leaderboard

Tap **Rankings** in the bottom nav to see:

- **Volunteer rankings** - sorted by total houses delivered
- **Your achievements** - badges you've unlocked

### Achievements

| Badge | Name | How to earn it |
|-------|------|---------------|
| First Delivery | Complete your first street |
| High Five | Complete 5 streets |
| Street Sweeper | Complete 10+ streets |
| Century Club | Deliver to 100+ houses |
| Postie Pro | Deliver to 500+ houses |
| Zone Champion | Help complete every street in a zone |
| Cross-Zone Crusader | Deliver in 3+ different zones |
| Ward Warrior | Deliver in all 8 zones |
