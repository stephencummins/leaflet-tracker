Casework Entries

When a canvasser logs a casework issue (a resident who raised a specific problem), it appears in the Casework section. Each entry shows:

  • Which group and road it came from, and which volunteer logged it
  • The resident's name and address (if provided)
  • A contact number or email (if provided)
  • The issue raised

To delete a casework entry, click the Delete button and confirm. This is permanent.

📌 Casework issues should be followed up and may be worth logging in Casewerk for full tracking.

For Canvassers

Canvassers use the main app (not this admin panel). Direct them to roadie.stephen8n.com where they can:

  • See their assigned road group
  • Mark roads as done
  • Log doorstep responses (Support / Against / Undecided / Not Home)
  • Log casework issues for residents who raise specific problems
